How it works

Six steps. One shorter list.

Every project runs the same way, whether it's a leaky faucet or your kid's entire birthday party.

01 · Give me the list

You hand it off

"Our deck needs repaired and I have no idea who to call." That's a complete request.

02 · Define "done"

We define the outcome

Budget, timeline, preferences, approval authority, and constraints, nailed down up front.

03 · Create the project

It becomes a task list

Research, outreach, site visits, proposals, comparisons: all mapped, all owned by us.

04 · Do the annoying part

We make the calls

Research, emails, follow-up, scheduling, coordination. The part you were avoiding.

05 · Return decisions

You get options, not emails

"Two quotes in. Option A vs. Option B, priced and scheduled. Reply A or B."

06 · Close the loop

We mark it handled

Work verified, loop closed. That feeling of closure is part of the product.
