We handle it.

Three projects, start to done.

The playset

A swing set, without the Saturday lost to it

The ask — a family wanted a play set up before summer, on a budget that ruled out buying new.

The work — we found a strong secondhand set on the marketplace, arranged the pickup, brought in painters to make it look new, then coordinated the install crew and the mulch delivery.

The outcome — a backyard centerpiece at a fraction of retail, and a weekend the parents got to keep.

The backyard

From bare yard to their favorite room in the house

The ask — a blank backyard, and a vision for a patio and pergola the client couldn't turn into contractor calls themselves.

The work — we sourced and vetted concrete contractors for the sidewalk and patio pour, found a pergola matched to the space, coordinated the install crew, then sourced furniture and decor around the client's style.

The outcome — a finished outdoor room, ready for the first cookout, with the client never sourcing a single quote.

The office

Out with the old, in on their terms

The ask — a home office that no longer fit how the client worked, and old furniture nobody wanted to deal with.

The work — we cleared the old setup — donating, trashing, or reselling for profit — brought in painters, sourced new furniture within budget, then proposed and installed decor to match their taste.

The outcome — a room they're proud to work from, handled without a single trip to a furniture store.

If something on your list sounds like this, that's exactly what we do.
