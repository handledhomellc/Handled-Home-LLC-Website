Portfolio

We handle it.

Three projects, start to done.

The playset

Backyard Upgrade

The ask: a family wanted a play set up before summer, on a budget that ruled out buying new.

The work: we sourced a strong secondhand set from the marketplace, handled the pickup, brought in painters to make it look brand new, then coordinated the install crew and mulch delivery.

The outcome: a like-new play set at a fraction of the cost, delivered and installed without the family lifting a finger.

The backyard

An Empty Yard, Turned Outdoor Oasis

The ask: an empty yard and a Pinterest board full of ideas, with no idea how to make the calls to bring it to life.

The work: we sourced and vetted concrete contractors for the sidewalk and patio pour, found a pergola matched to the space, coordinated the install crew, then sourced furniture and decor around the client's style.

The outcome: an outdoor oasis that matches the vision, without the client making a single call.

The office

An Outdated Office, Turned Workspace They're Proud Of

The ask: an office overdue for a change, and a client stuck on the first step: where to even start.

The work: we cleared the old setup — donating, trashing, or reselling for profit — brought in painters, sourced new furniture within budget, then proposed and installed decor to match their taste.

The outcome: a workspace overhaul, start to finish, without the client lifting a finger.
